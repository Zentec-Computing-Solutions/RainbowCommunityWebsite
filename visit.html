The road to Rainbow
A little off
the beaten track.
We’re 11 km from Tākaka at the end of McCallum Road in the Anatoki Valley.
  1. Arrange your visit with us before setting off.
  2. Use 688 McCallum Road as your destination.
  3. The last 5 km is narrow, single-lane gravel. Take it slowly; around 30 km/h is advised by the community.
