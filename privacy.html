A little clarity

Privacy & cookies.

How this website works, and what happens when you get in touch.

Using this website

This static website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, or local storage. Fonts and photographs are served with the website. The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and server logs, to operate the service.

Making an enquiry

The enquiry form prepares an email on your device. It does not submit information to a website server. Your details are shared with Rainbow Valley only when you choose to send the message through your email provider.

Your information

When you contact Rainbow Valley, the community may receive your name, email address, travel plans, and any other information you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to you and arrange the services you request.

The community’s published policy says personal information is not sold, rented, loaned, or traded. Information may be shared where needed to provide a requested service or where legally required, and retained for the necessary service period and applicable legal obligations.

External websites

Links to maps and other services take you to external websites with their own privacy practices. Those services are only opened when you follow a link.

Questions or corrections

For questions about your personal information, or to request a correction, email info@rainbowcommunity.org.nz or call +64 27 717 4271.
