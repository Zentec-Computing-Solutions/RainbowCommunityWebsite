# Content and image sources

Reviewed 17 November 2026. Copy has been condensed and rewritten for this redesign; photographs are retained from the original community website.

- [Rainbow Valley homepage](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/): identity, setting, season, and contact details.
- [Accommodation](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/golden-bay-backpacker-accommodation.php): guesthouse, cabin, facilities, seasonal closure, and road information.
- [Camping](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/golden-bay-camping.php): small campground, facilities, and advance enquiries.
- [Community](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/rainbow-valley-community-golden-bay.php): shared life and community values.
- [Farm](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/rainbow-valley-community-farm.php): farming approach, animals, and plantings.
- [History](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/rainbow-valley-community-history.php): the valley and the community’s founding in spring 1974.
- [Visitors](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/rainbow-valley-community-visitors.php): visits and volunteering by arrangement.
- [Activities](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/rainbow-valley-backpackers-activities.php): river, walks, gold panning, and local activities.
- [Contact](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/contact-golden-bay-backpackers-accommodation.php): address, email, telephone, and directions.
- [Published privacy policy](https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/takaka-accommodation-cookies-privacy.php): the community’s handling of personal information. The new privacy page also describes this implementation’s actual behaviour.

Photographs in `assets/images/` retain their original filenames and come from `https://rainbowcommunity.org.nz/imgs/`. Photo ownership remains with the original rights holders. The rainbow mark and SVG icons were created for this redesign.

DM Sans and DM Serif Display come from the Google Fonts project. Their SIL Open Font License files are included with the local font files.
